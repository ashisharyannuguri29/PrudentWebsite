Automotive

Vehicles are becoming software platforms and factories are becoming data systems. We help automotive organizations connect product, plant and customer data so teams can move faster and decide with confidence.

Challenges we solve

  • Connected vehicle and telematics data growing faster than platforms can handle
  • Disconnected plant, supply chain and dealer systems
  • Rising software complexity across long product cycles
  • Customer journeys split between sales, service and ownership

How we help

  • Build cloud data platforms for connected vehicle and plant data
  • Modernize legacy manufacturing and dealer applications
  • Apply analytics and AI to quality, demand and maintenance
  • Secure vehicle, plant and customer data end to end

Typical focus areas

Connected platforms Data & analytics Cloud Intelligent automation

Challenges we solve

  • Growing regulatory, risk and security demands
  • Fragmented customer data across channels
  • Fraud and operational risk that keep evolving

How we help

  • Modernize applications and integrate legacy systems safely
  • Strengthen security monitoring and risk management
  • Unify customer data for personalization and insight
  • Apply AI and analytics to risk, fraud and service

Typical focus areas

Cybersecurity Risk management AI Application modernization

  • Aging applications that are hard to change

How we help

  • Integrate clinical and operational data on modern platforms
  • Design security and access controls into every system
  • Modernize and migrate legacy healthcare applications
  • Automate routine administrative workflows

Data engineering Application modernization Security Intelligent automation
