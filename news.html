TECHNOLOGY September 2026

Businesses accelerate their digital transformation

Organizations are moving faster to modernize platforms and processes.

Digital transformation is shifting from large, one-time programs to continuous improvement. Leaders are breaking work into smaller releases that deliver value early and let teams learn as they go.

The common thread is a stronger link between business goals and technology choices. Teams that define outcomes first, then pick platforms and partners, tend to avoid costly rework.

Key takeaways

  • Start with outcomes, not tools
  • Release in smaller steps and learn quickly
  • Keep security and reliability in scope from day one
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE September 2026

Organizations explore new possibilities with AI

Practical AI use cases are moving from pilots into production.

Many organizations have moved past experimentation and now ask how AI fits into everyday work. The most useful projects target a specific process, such as service, reporting or document handling.

Trusted data and clear ownership matter as much as the model itself. Teams that plan for monitoring, access and review from the start find it easier to scale.

Key takeaways

  • Pick use cases tied to a measurable goal
  • Invest in data quality and governance
  • Plan for monitoring and human review
SUSTAINABILITY September 2026

Technology supports sustainable business growth

Data and cloud help teams track and reduce their footprint.

Businesses want to understand their environmental impact and act on it. Modern data platforms make it easier to collect, combine and report the information that sustainability goals depend on.

Efficient cloud use and better planning also reduce waste. Treating sustainability as a design requirement, rather than a report, helps it become part of everyday decisions.

Key takeaways

  • Measure first so progress is visible
  • Use cloud and data to reduce waste
  • Build sustainability into design decisions
DATA & ANALYTICS September 2026

Data platforms turn information into faster decisions

Unified, trusted data is becoming the base for analytics and AI.

As data volumes grow, leaders want one reliable view rather than many competing reports. Consolidating pipelines and agreeing on definitions reduces debate and speeds up decisions.

A solid data foundation also prepares the ground for analytics and AI. Governance, quality checks and clear ownership keep that foundation dependable over time.

Key takeaways

  • Agree on shared definitions and ownership
  • Automate pipelines and quality checks
  • Treat data as a product teams can rely on
